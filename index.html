About Us

NayePankh Foundation works towards education, empowerment and social development.

By UP GOVERNMENT, 80G & 12A Registered NGO


"NayePankh Foundation" is a non governmental organisation with a strong desire to help the society and make it a better place for all, by doing everything in our power and to make our vision successful we would require your vital support. Service to mankind is the service to god. Let’s revolutionise the society together!.

Our Programs

Education

We provide quality education, learning resources, and guidance to help children and youth build a brighter future through knowledge and skills.

Women Empowerment

We support women through awareness programs, leadership training, and opportunities that promote independence, confidence, and equal participation in society.

Skill Development

We offer practical training and career-oriented workshops that help individuals develop professional skills and improve employment opportunities.
